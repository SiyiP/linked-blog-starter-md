Med Name 
amoxicillin  (and clavulanate)

Mechanism 
competitive inhibitor of penicillin-binding protein 1 - 
- penicillin binds proteins responsible for glycosyltransferase + transpeptidase rxns leading to cross-linking of d-ala and d-aspartate ; inhibition of bacterial cell wall synthesis 
- clavulanate potassium = beta lactamase inhibitor, preventing some bacteria to gain resistance 

Dose = based on amoxicillin component. 
= neonates oral 15 mg amoxicillin/kg/dose q12h 
- IV neonates 25 mg amox/kg/dose q12h
- for pediatrics, standard is typically <= 45 mg/kg/day 
	- in forms of tablet, suspension 

Indications 
bite wound infection - 40-50 mg kg/day q8-12h 
impetigo - 25-45 mg/kg/day , 7 days 
osteo - 80-120 mg/kg/day q6-8 
otitis media aom - in standard 40-45 mg/kg/day q8-12h 
	typically 10 days 
pneumonia - empiric / step down = 80-90 mg/kg/day q8-12h 
duration is 5 days likely adequate for uncomplicated dz 

Pharmacokinetics 
immediate and extended release forms 
- t-max 1-1.5 hr 
- half life is 1.2 hr for amox, 1 hr clavulanic 
- amox - predominantly renal 
- clavulanate - renal and nonrenal metabolism 

SEs-  
- diarrhea - can occur 2 months + after stopped 
- n/v often dose related 
NIcU - 4:1
