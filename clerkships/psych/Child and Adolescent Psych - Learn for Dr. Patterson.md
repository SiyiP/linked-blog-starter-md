		- 5-8 yo = when theory of mind develops for a child F develops theory of mind earlier vs later -

- assessment - every assessment has 3 portions; **each one is different based on if or if not the pt has theory of mind**
		- if <8 yo, no theory of mind. if they're at the psych , they have something delaying it. 
		- older than 10 = assume they do have theory of mind. 
		- **bt 8-10 = based on complaint, assume whetehr or not they have theory of mind; like externalizing symptoms like tantrums, etc. = assume they DONT. internalizing symptoms of depression, self-harm, not talking to friends = assume they have theory of mind.**
		- you can see if they have theory of mind typically by talking about their social life, what they're learning in school etc. 
			- my friend was sad on playground -> if no theory "she was sad bc she was crying"; but if there is theory "she was sad bc she was made fun of"= more of a **universalizing "if this happened to me, I'd be sad."**

		- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">if they have theory of mind, esp adolescent - this is the most important rapport-based decision to interview them alone FIRST.</mark>
			- teens are more honest on avg than adults in drs interviews. spend like 35 min for rapport and information 
			- they also don't prioritize family as much; starting 10-15 or 12 yo, it's important
